What we ask of you

Two ways to join.

Pick whichever fits you, or mix them. Either way you get your own storefront and keep 50% of what it earns. You never have to buy anything.

  • Option 1 · Model it yourself. Pick 2–3 pieces you already own and love that are sold on Amazon, and post at least one video of yourself wearing each one, in your own style. We'll also make you a free S, M and XL try-on clip of the same piece as a bonus ("here's how it looks on other sizes").
  • Option 2 · Let our AI model it. Pick any 2–3 pieces on Amazon you'd love to recommend, even ones you don't own. We make free try-on clips of each piece on three body types (S, M and XL), and you post them on your TikTok or Instagram.

Either way

That's it. No posting schedule, no minimum sales, and you can stop anytime.

The details

Simple and fair.

  • 50/50 split. Your page has its own tracking ID, so every sale from it is counted to you.
  • Monthly payouts via PayPal or Venmo once you're over $10. Amazon pays out about 60 days after each month ends.
  • Free try-on clips of up to 3 of your picks. They're labeled as AI-generated, and posts should be tagged #ad.
  • A feature on Glamour Daily or Glamour Nightly.
  • Anything wrong? A broken link, a page issue, a payout question: tell us and we'll fix it right away.
  • Share your page link, not individual Amazon links. That's how your sales get credited to you.
